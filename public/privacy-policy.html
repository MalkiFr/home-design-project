Privacy Policy

Home Design AI

Last updated: September 3, 2026

1. About the Application

Home Design AI is a prototype application designed to help users discover and explore home design inspiration based on their preferences. The current prototype integrates Pinterest content to demonstrate Pinterest-related functionality.

2. Information We Collect

The current prototype does not require users to create an account or provide personal information directly to us.

When Pinterest content is displayed through Pinterest's services, Pinterest or other third parties may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

3. How We Use Information

Any information processed by the application is used only for the purposes of operating, testing, and improving the prototype and its integration with Pinterest.

4. Third-Party Services

The application may display content provided by Pinterest. Pinterest is a third-party service, and its processing of information is governed by Pinterest's own policies and terms.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the application. The prototype does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application develops and additional functionality is introduced.

7. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the application owner through the contact information associated with the Home Design AI project.
